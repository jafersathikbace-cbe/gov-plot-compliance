# Compliance workflow

A policy template defines milestone rules such as a title, type, due interval, required value, unit, and evidence requirement. When a case is activated, the lifecycle service creates the corresponding milestone records.

Milestones move through application-defined states such as pending, due soon, overdue, and verified. The case status is derived from those milestone states and the legal status of the case.

Evidence submissions are reviewed by authorized officers. Incentive claims are linked to cases and can be reviewed and approved by authorized roles. Legal actions are separately restricted to administrative roles and are recorded in the case history.
